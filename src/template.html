How to use this page
  1. Enter your account value and cash available below.
  2. Add each open trade with + Add trade. Pick the strategy, then fill in the strikes, expiration, number of contracts and the prices you were filled at.
  3. Type the current stock price and current option prices from your broker. Once automatic prices are switched on, most tickers fill in by themselves when you press Refresh prices.
  4. Read Needs attention first, then Account exposure.
  5. Open Alert settings and change the limits to match your own rules.

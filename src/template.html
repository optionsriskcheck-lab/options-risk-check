Your trades are private to this browser

  • Everything you enter here is saved only in this web browser, on this device. It is never sent anywhere: not to whoever made or shared this page, and not to anyone else.
  • Anyone else who opens this link gets their own empty page. They can't see your trades, and you can't see theirs.
  • Your trades won't show up on your phone, another computer, or a different browser on this computer.
  • Private or incognito windows erase everything when you close them. Use a normal browser window.
  • Clearing your browser's history or site data also erases them. Press Save to file now and then to keep a backup. Load from file brings your trades back, or moves them to another device.

How to use this page
  1. Enter your account value and cash available below.
  2. Add each open trade with + Add trade. Pick the strategy, then fill in the strikes, expiration, number of contracts and the prices you were filled at.
  3. Prices fill in by themselves for the S&P 500 plus SPY, QQQ, IWM and EWZ. Press Refresh prices for the newest. For anything else, type the current stock price and current option prices from your broker.
  4. Read Needs attention first, then Account exposure.
  5. Open Alert settings and change the limits to match your own rules.

Want to see how it works first? Use More ▾ → Load example trades. Your own trades are set aside while you look, and one button brings them back.
